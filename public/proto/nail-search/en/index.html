How to use

About the service

✨ The National Assembly Library now ‘understands’ and ‘answers’ your questions ✨

Moving beyond keyword matching, it reads the meaning and context of your question to find the best-fitting material. Ask in sentences, as you would a reference librarian. It distils the key points from a vast collection and shows the source documents behind them.

Material available

Public users

Full texts of public-policy material — social issues and bills, research reports, ministry reports and Assembly publications — searchable by meaning.

Assembly staff

For legislative work, every holding of the Assembly Library (laws, bills, minutes, domestic and international papers, expert reports, statistics) is searchable by meaning.

How it works

① Ask
  • Type what you’re looking for as a natural sentence.

e.g. “Recent Assembly debate on environmental regulation”, “Latest policy reports on youth employment”

② Semantic search
  • The AI analyses the meaning and intent of your question to find the most relevant material.
  • It also explores material connected by context, even when the keywords don’t match.
③ Check answers & sources
  • Each paragraph of the summary carries a number— tap it to see the source material.
  • Widen the topic with suggested questions and keywords to keep exploring.
